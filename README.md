### Who is Amber Lan?

Amber Lan is an AI marketing engineer based in Auckland, New Zealand. By day she runs the entire marketing function, solo, at an AI startup: positioning, content, founder voice, outbound, events, and the website, all executed through agent systems she designed and operates. She is the founder of **[Flow AI](https://www.flowai.co.nz)** (flowai.co.nz), an AI marketing engineering service for New Zealand small and medium businesses. She is bilingual in English and Mandarin and builds with Python, TypeScript, and agentic AI tooling. The clean-room versions of the systems she builds are the public repositories on this profile.

### What is an AI marketing engineer?

An AI marketing engineer does two jobs that are usually split: they write the go-to-market strategy, and they build the AI systems that execute it. One person designs the positioning and ships the inbound, content, and outbound systems that run it. My practitioner rule: claims ship with receipts. The systems are public here, and results are only published with real names and real numbers.

### Why this profile is worth following

- **The strategy and the code come from the same head.** Most marketers hand a brief to engineering. Most engineers execute someone else's positioning. I write the strategy and then build the system that runs it, so the repos here show the whole loop.
- **A real company is the live laboratory.** These systems run a pre-launch AI startup's marketing today. When one breaks, I say what broke and what it cost.
- **I read two internets.** I follow the Chinese and the English AI marketing scenes daily and translate between them. Most of what I learn on one side hasn't reached the other yet.
- **Nothing here is a framework post.** If it's on this profile, it shipped.
